Best cruises from Florida for families

For a family cruise from Florida, start with short Bahamas sailings of three to four nights from Port Canaveral or Miami, then choose the line whose kids program and ship fit your children's ages.

Quick take

  • Start with3 to 4 nights
  • PortsPort Canaveral or Miami
  • Kids club (Carnival)Camp Ocean, ages 2 to 11
  • CheckKids program ages

Florida is the easiest place in the country to start cruising with kids. Short sailings leave all week, ships are big, and you can pick a port that fits your flights.

Start short

A 3 or 4 night Bahamas cruise is a good test. You see how your kids handle the ship and the pool without committing to a week. From Port Canaveral those sailings stop at places like Nassau, Perfect Day at CocoCay, and Great Stirrup Cay.

Lines to compare

Disney Cruise LinePort Canaveral, 3 to 7 nights, dedicated terminal
Royal CaribbeanPort Canaveral and Miami, big ships including Icon of the Seas
CarnivalBoth ports, Camp Ocean included in the fare, ages 2 to 11
MSC and NorwegianBoth ports, large ships with many pools

Kids programs, age groups, and any extra fees differ by line, so check each one's current rules for your children's ages. Avoid Virgin Voyages, which is adults only.

What to check

  • The kids club age groups and hours
  • Whether your children need a minimum age for particular activities
  • Gratuities, which add to the fare
  • Where the ship docks and how you will get there
Book the room, not just the fare

Families often need a bigger cabin or connecting rooms. Ask your advisor to price a few cabin types side by side.

Common questions

What is the best cruise from Florida for families?

It depends on your kids' ages, but a 3 or 4 night Bahamas sailing from Port Canaveral or Miami is a good first choice.

What age is the Carnival kids club?

Camp Ocean starts at age 2 and covers 2 to 11, included in the fare.

Is Virgin Voyages good for kids?

No. It is adults only.
