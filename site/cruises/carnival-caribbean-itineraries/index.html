Carnival Caribbean cruises: itineraries, ports, and where to sail from

Carnival sails the Caribbean and Bahamas from ports including Miami, Port Canaveral, and Norfolk, Virginia, on 4 to 7 night itineraries that stop at ports like Nassau, Cozumel, St. Thomas, and Carnival's own Celebration Key, and the best choice is usually the port you can drive to.

The itinerary decides what your cruise feels like, and the departure port decides what it costs to get there. Carnival covers the Caribbean from several US ports, so the useful question is not which island is best but which sailing fits your time, your budget, and your drive. Here is how the main options work.

Bahamas, Eastern, and Western Caribbean

Carnival's Caribbean sailings fall into three groups. Bahamas cruises are the shortest, usually 4 or 5 nights, with stops like Nassau and Carnival's own beach destinations, which makes them the easiest first cruise. Eastern Caribbean sailings run about a week and call at ports such as St. Thomas, St. Maarten, San Juan, Grand Turk, and Amber Cove in the Dominican Republic, which suits travelers who like shopping and variety. Western Caribbean sailings call at places like Cozumel in Mexico, and they suit snorkeling, ruins, and beach days.

Carnival's private Bahamas destinations

Two stops are worth knowing by name. Celebration Key is Carnival's newer exclusive destination on Grand Bahama, and Half Moon Cay, which Carnival now brands as RelaxAway, Half Moon Cay, is its long-running private island. Visits to both are part of your fare with no extra charge to go ashore, though some activities, food, and drinks there may cost extra. They are the reason a short Bahamas sailing can feel like a full beach vacation.

Sailing from Miami

Carnival Celebration is based in PortMiami on 7-day Eastern and Western Caribbean itineraries, according to Carnival's published 2027 and 2028 schedule. Eastern routes include Celebration Key, Amber Cove, Grand Turk, St. Thomas, St. Maarten, San Juan, RelaxAway, Half Moon Cay, and Nassau, and Western routes include Celebration Key and Cozumel. Miami has the most flights and the widest choice of sailings, so it is the best port if you are flying in.

Sailing from Port Canaveral

Port Canaveral, on Florida's east coast, is the cruise port closest to Orlando, which makes it easy to pair a cruise with theme parks. Mardi Gras sails from here, and from late May 2027 it moves to 4 and 5 day Bahamas cruises calling at Celebration Key, RelaxAway, Half Moon Cay, and Nassau depending on the length. If you are building a Disney or Universal trip too, see our Orlando on a budget guide.

Sailing from Norfolk, Virginia

For anyone in Virginia, the Carolinas, or the Mid-Atlantic, Norfolk is the shortcut, because you drive to the ship and skip the airfare. Carnival began year-round sailings from Norfolk's Half Moone Cruise Center in February 2025 with Carnival Sunshine, and its schedule includes the Bahamas, Bermuda, and the Eastern Caribbean. Carnival scheduled Sunshine to add Celebration Key to its calls from September 2025. Carnival Sunshine runs through mid-May 2027, and Carnival Freedom replaces it from May 15, 2027 with 33 cruises that Carnival says will include the Bahamas, Bermuda, the Eastern Caribbean, and Canada. As a price sample, a 6-day Bermuda sailing from Norfolk in September 2025 priced at $782 for an inside cabin and $1,259 for a balcony. Budget for terminal parking on top of the fare.

If you live within driving distance of Norfolk, a drive-to sailing skips the airfare, which can change which cruise is the better deal.

When to go

Hurricane season runs from June to November, and fares are often lower then, with itineraries sometimes changed to dodge storms. Winter is peak season with the highest fares. Our first Caribbean cruise guide covers timing in more detail, and the Carnival ships guide helps you pick the ship that sails your route. Schedules and ship assignments change, so confirm the details on Carnival's site before you book.

Common questions

Does Carnival sail from Norfolk, Virginia?

Yes. Carnival Sunshine has sailed year-round from Norfolk since February 2025, and Carnival Freedom takes over from May 15, 2027.

What is the best Carnival itinerary for a first cruise?

A 4 or 5 night Bahamas sailing. It is short, inexpensive, and includes a beach day at a Carnival destination such as Celebration Key.

Is Celebration Key free to visit?

Going ashore is included in your cruise fare. Some food, drinks, and activities there cost extra.

Which Carnival port is closest to Orlando?

Port Canaveral, on Florida's east coast, is the cruise port closest to Orlando's theme parks.
