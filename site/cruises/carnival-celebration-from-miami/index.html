Carnival Celebration cruise: the short version

Carnival Celebration is one of the biggest ships you can sail from Miami, and it runs Caribbean cruises there all year.

Celebration joined the fleet in November 2022 as a sister of Mardi Gras and Jubilee, Carnival's Excel-class ships. It carries well over five thousand guests, so expect a lively, busy ship with a lot to do for kids, teens, and adults. Most of its Miami cruises are seven nights.

Carnival Celebration itinerary: where it goes from Miami

Celebration sails round trips from the Port of Miami, and the late November 2026 week is a good example: St. Thomas, Puerto Plata (Amber Cove in the Dominican Republic), and Celebration Key, Carnival's own destination in the Bahamas. Two eight-night sailings are listed for spring 2027, on March 28 and April 24. Ports change from one sailing to the next, so ask for the exact route on your dates.

Pools and water fun on Carnival Celebration

Water is a big part of the day on this ship. Carnival WaterWorks is the largest water park in the fleet, with three spiral slides, a two-lane racing slide, and a tipping bucket. There are also the Beach Pool, the Patio Pool, and the Tides Pool, an infinity pool at the back of the ship with two hot tubs. Guests in Havana cabins get their own Havana pool area, and there is another pool at Loft 19.

How much does a Carnival Celebration cruise cost?

CruiseMapper lists Celebration sailings from about $443 up to about $1,331 per person, for two people sharing a cabin. The late November 2026 week was listed from about $908 per person, and the most expensive date on the list was the Christmas week, December 27, 2026. Holiday and school-break weeks cost the most.

The fare is only part of the bill. Add taxes and port fees, daily gratuities (about $17 per person per day for a standard cabin), drinks, specialty dining, and excursions. Prices change daily, so treat the number above as a starting point, not a quote.

Is Carnival Celebration good for kids and families?

For most families, yes. Celebration has Camp Ocean, Carnival's kids program, included in the fare for ages 2 to 11, plus Circle C for ages 12 to 14 and Club O2 for ages 15 to 17. There is also Family Harbor, a family-focused cabin area with its own lounge, and about 300 connecting or family cabin pairs. Babies and toddlers can come along, but this is a big, busy ship, so a quieter vessel may suit very young kids better. Compare options in our Carnival ships guide.

Where to eat on Carnival Celebration

Dinner in the main restaurant and the buffet are included, and there are many other places to eat, from burgers and pizza to a sushi bar and a seafood shack. Some are free and some cost extra, so look at the box above, then ask which ones are included on your sailing. Make reservations for the paid restaurants before you sail, since popular times fill up.

Carnival Celebration from Miami: how to get to the ship

The ship sails from the Port of Miami, so flying in the day before is the safest plan. Our Miami cruise guide covers the port, and our Port Canaveral vs Miami guide helps if you are still choosing where to sail from.

Who should book Carnival Celebration

Pick Celebration if you want a big, high-energy ship with lots of water fun, if you have school-age kids or teens, or if you want a Caribbean cruise that starts in Miami. Common questions

Where does Carnival Celebration sail from?

Carnival Celebration is based at the Port of Miami, Florida, and sails Caribbean round trips from there all year. Most are seven nights.

How much does a Carnival Celebration cruise cost?

CruiseMapper lists sailings from about $443 to $1,331 per person for two sharing a cabin. A seven-night sailing in late November 2026 was listed from about $908 per person. Add taxes, gratuities, and extras, and check the live price for your dates.

How big is Carnival Celebration?

It is about 183,900 gross tons and 1,132 feet long, with 2,641 cabins and room for 5,374 to 6,338 guests, depending on how many share each cabin.

Does Carnival Celebration have a roller coaster?

Yes. The Bolt roller coaster is part of The Ultimate Playground on the top decks, along with a ropes course and other activities. Check ride rules on Carnival's site.

Is Carnival Celebration good for kids?

Yes. Camp Ocean is included in the fare for ages 2 to 11, there are lounges for tweens and teens, and the water park is the biggest on any Carnival ship. Check age and height rules before you book.

Where does Carnival Celebration go?

A late November 2026 week visits St. Thomas, Puerto Plata (Amber Cove), and Celebration Key. Two eight-night sailings are listed for March 28 and April 24, 2027. Ports vary by sailing, so check your date.
