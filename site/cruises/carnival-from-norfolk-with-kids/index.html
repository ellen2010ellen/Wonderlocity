Carnival from Norfolk with kids: a cruise with no flight

Carnival sails year-round from Norfolk, Virginia, so a Virginia Beach family can drive to the ship, skip the airfares, and park for $15 a day, with Carnival Sunshine through mid-May 2027 and Carnival Freedom after that.

Quick take

  • Ship nowCarnival Sunshine until mid-May 2027
  • Ship afterCarnival Freedom from May 15, 2027
  • Parking$15 per day, free shuttle
  • Kids clubCamp Ocean, ages 2 to 11

The best part of cruising from Norfolk is what you leave out: no flights, no checked-bag fees, no rental car at the other end. You pack the car, drive to the ship, and walk on. For a family of four, that can change which cruise is the better deal, and it makes a short Bahamas or Eastern Caribbean sailing much easier to try.

Which ship you will sail on

Carnival Sunshine has been Norfolk's ship and stays through mid-May 2027, finishing 21 cruises before it moves on. It entered service in 1996 as Carnival Destiny and was rebuilt and renamed in 2013. Carnival Freedom arrives on May 15, 2027 and runs 33 cruises from Norfolk for the rest of that year. It carries about 3,000 guests and entered service in 2006. For 2027, Carnival lists Bahamas, Bermuda, Eastern Caribbean, and Canada sailings from Norfolk, so check which itinerary and length fits your school break.

Parking, step by step

The Half Moone Cruise Terminal is at 1 Waterside Drive in downtown Norfolk. The City of Norfolk lists Cedar Grove parking at 1000 Monticello Avenue, about 1.5 miles away, at $15 per day, and you can pre-pay or pay on entry by cash or card. A free shuttle takes you to the terminal, and you can check your luggage at the lot so it goes straight to the ship. On cruise days the lot opens at 9:30 a.m., shuttles start around 9:45 a.m., and the last shuttle runs at 2:30 p.m. If you would rather skip parking, drop-off is on Waterside Drive.

Do the real math

Parking runs $15 a day, so a week costs about $105 to $120. Compare that with four airfares plus bags to a Florida port. Add gratuities to any fare you compare.

What kids do on board

Carnival's kids program, Camp Ocean, is included in the cruise fare and starts at age 2. It splits kids into Penguins (2 to 5), Stingrays (6 to 8), and Sharks (9 to 11). A late-evening option called Night Owls has a fee. Our Carnival ships guide explains how older and newer ships differ, which matters when you choose between Sunshine and Freedom.

Before you go

Every traveler, kids included, needs proper travel documents at embarkation, and guests without them cannot board, so confirm Carnival's current rules for your group. Carnival also prohibits all speakers, including Bluetooth, and holds them until the cruise ends, so leave the kids' speakers at home. Our family cruise packing list has a printable checklist.

Make a day of Norfolk

Nauticus, the maritime museum on the terminal campus beside the USS Wisconsin, offers cruise passengers buy-one-get-one-free admission. It is a good use of an early arrival or a post-cruise afternoon.
