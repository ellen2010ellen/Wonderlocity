Carnival Horizon cruise: the short version

Carnival Horizon is a mid-size Carnival ship with a Dr. Seuss water park and a bike ride in the sky, based in Miami.

Horizon entered service in 2018 and has sailed from Miami year-round since September 2018. At about 133,600 gross tons it is smaller than Carnival's newest giants, but it still carries nearly four thousand guests and packs in a lot: an aerial pedal ride, a ropes course, an IMAX theater, and a kids program themed around Dr. Seuss. From 2027 it also sails some cruises from Galveston, Texas.

Carnival Horizon itinerary: where it goes from Miami

From Miami, Horizon sails six-night and eight-night Caribbean round trips. A sample six-night week in November 2026 visited Celebration Key in the Bahamas, Montego Bay in Jamaica, and Grand Cayman. A sample eight-night week the same month went south to Aruba, Bonaire, and Curacao. Miami sailings run into April 2027 on the current schedule, so check which route fits your dates.

SkyRide, slides, and IMAX: the big attractions

SkyRide is a pedal-powered ride on a track about 800 feet long, roughly 150 feet above the sea. Dr. Seuss WaterWorks has the Cat in the Hat, a 450-foot raft slide, and Fun Things, a 213-foot body slide. Inside, Carnival's IMAX theater has a screen three decks high, and there is a 5D Thrill Theater. Check age, height, and weight rules for each attraction on Carnival's site.

How much does a Carnival Horizon cruise cost?

CruiseMapper lists Horizon sailings from Miami from about $387 (a six-night week in January 2027) up to about $1,062 (an eight-night sailing over Christmas, December 27, 2026). A sample eight-night southern Caribbean week in late November 2026 was listed from about $869, and a six-night week from about $438. Holiday weeks cost the most.

Add taxes and port fees, daily gratuities (about $17 per person per day for a standard cabin), drinks, specialty dining, and excursions. Prices change daily, so use these numbers as a starting point, not a quote.

Is Carnival Horizon good for kids and families?

Yes. Horizon is built around the Dr. Seuss at Sea program, with Dr. Seuss Bookville, Seuss at Sea breakfasts, and the Seuss-a-Palooza Parade, along with Camp Ocean, which is included in the fare for ages 2 to 11, and the Circle C and Club O2 lounges for older kids and teens. Family Harbor has 86 cabins near a lounge with snacks and TVs. Many cabins are 185 to 220 square feet, so bring fewer bags if there are four of you. Compare ships in our Carnival ships guide.

Nights out: shows, bars, and the casino

The 1,400-seat Liquid Lounge hosts Playlist Productions shows such as Celestial Strings, Soulbound, and Vintage Pop, and Amor Cubano plays in the Havana Bar. There is a 484-seat casino, the Limelight Lounge, and a large LED sculpture called Dreamscape in the atrium.

Carnival Horizon from Miami: how to get to the ship

Horizon sails from the Port of Miami, so fly in the day before if you can. Our Miami cruise guide covers the port, and Port Canaveral vs Miami helps you choose between Florida ports.

Who should book Carnival Horizon

Pick Horizon if you want a lively family ship with kid-friendly extras like Dr. Seuss, slides, and the SkyRide, and you do not need the newest, biggest ship. Want a roller coaster at sea? See Carnival Mardi Gras or Celebration. Skip it if you want a calm cruise. Tell me your dates and who is traveling, and I will compare Horizon with other sailings.

Common questions

Where does Carnival Horizon sail from?

Carnival Horizon has been based in Miami, Florida, year-round since September 2018. It also sails from Galveston, Texas, on a schedule that begins in May 2027.

How much does a Carnival Horizon cruise cost?

CruiseMapper lists Miami sailings from about $387 to $1,062 per person for two sharing a cabin. A six-night week in November 2026 was listed from about $438, and an eight-night week from about $869. Add taxes, gratuities, and extras.

How big is Carnival Horizon?

It is about 133,600 gross tons and 1,056 feet long, with 1,965 cabins and room for 3,960 to 4,716 guests.

Does Carnival Horizon have water slides?

Yes. Dr. Seuss WaterWorks on Deck 12 has the Cat in the Hat raft slide and the Fun Things body slide.

Is Carnival Horizon good for kids?

Yes. It has the Dr. Seuss at Sea program, Camp Ocean for ages 2 to 11 included in the fare, Circle C and Club O2 lounges, and Family Harbor cabins. Check age and height rules for rides before you book.

How long are Carnival Horizon cruises from Miami?

Miami sailings are six or eight nights. Galveston sailings are mostly four or five nights, with one eight-night sailing in May 2027.
