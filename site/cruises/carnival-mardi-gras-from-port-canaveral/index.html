Carnival Mardi Gras cruise: the short version

Mardi Gras is Carnival's biggest, boldest ship, and it sails from Port Canaveral, so you can pair a Carnival Mardi Gras cruise with an Orlando theme park week.

It entered service in 2021 as the first ship of Carnival's Excel class, and it was the first cruise ship in the world with a roller coaster. Today it runs Bahamas and Caribbean cruises from Port Canaveral. That makes it one of the easiest big-ship cruises to reach from Orlando, and an easy add-on to a Disney or Universal trip.

The Bolt roller coaster at sea

Bolt is the headline of the ship. It is a roller coaster built on the top deck, and it is the reason many families pick Mardi Gras over older Carnival ships. Check the current height and age rules for Bolt and every other ride on Carnival's site before you promise anything to the kids.

Carnival Mardi Gras itinerary: where it goes

In late 2026, Mardi Gras sails Caribbean round trips from Port Canaveral, mostly four to seven nights. A seven-night sailing in late November calls at Nassau, Half Moon Cay, Puerto Plata (Amber Cove), and Celebration Key. In 2027 Carnival adds shorter Bahamas cruises that are easier on a calendar and a budget, and the details are in the itinerary cards below.

How much does a Carnival Mardi Gras cruise cost?

CruiseMapper lists Mardi Gras sailings from about $390 up to about $1,505 per person, for two people sharing a cabin. The price depends on how many nights you sail and what week you choose. A seven-night sailing from late November 2026 was listed from about $1,014 per person, which is a good reminder that holiday and school-break weeks cost more than quiet weeks.

Fares are only part of the cost. Add taxes and port fees, daily gratuities, drinks and specialty dining, excursions, and parking if you drive to Port Canaveral. Port Canaveral charges $20 per day plus tax for parking. Fares change daily, so the number above is a place to start, not a quote.

Is Carnival Mardi Gras good for kids and families?

Yes, for most families. The ship is big enough to keep kids busy every day: the roller coaster, pools, and slides, plus Camp Ocean, Carnival's kids program, included in the fare for ages 2 to 11. It splits kids into Penguins (2 to 5), Stingrays (6 to 8), and Sharks (9 to 11). For toddlers and babies, a quieter ship may suit you better. Read our Carnival ships guide to compare.

Carnival Mardi Gras from Orlando: how to get to the ship

Port Canaveral is about 45 minutes east of Orlando International Airport. Most families rent a car, take a shuttle, or book a rideshare or a private transfer. Fly in the day before so a late flight cannot make you miss the ship. Our Port Canaveral guide covers parking, transfers, and what to do nearby, and our family cruise guide shows how Mardi Gras compares with other Florida ships.

Who should book Carnival Mardi Gras

Pick Mardi Gras if you want a big, lively ship with a lot to do, if you are cruising with school-age kids or teens, or if you want a cruise from Orlando without a long drive. Common questions

Where does Carnival Mardi Gras sail from?

Carnival Mardi Gras is based at Port Canaveral, Florida, about 45 minutes east of Orlando International Airport. In 2026 it sails Bahamas and Caribbean round trips, mostly four to seven nights.

How much does a Carnival Mardi Gras cruise cost?

Sailings listed by CruiseMapper range from about $390 to $1,505 per person for two sharing a cabin, depending on the length and the date. A seven-night sailing in late November 2026 was listed from about $1,014 per person. Always add taxes, gratuities, and extras, and check the live price for your dates.

Does Carnival Mardi Gras have a roller coaster?

Yes. Mardi Gras was the first cruise ship with a roller coaster, called Bolt, on its top deck.

Is Carnival Mardi Gras good for kids?

It is a big ship with plenty to do, and Carnival's Camp Ocean kids program is included in the fare for ages 2 to 11. Check height rules for any ride or activity, and confirm the current kids program details on Carnival's site before you book.

How long are Carnival Mardi Gras cruises in 2027?

Carnival lists three seven-night sailings from Port Canaveral on May 1, 8, and 15, 2027, then five-night Bahamas cruises from May 22, 2027, and four-night Bahamas cruises from May 27, 2027.

Can I drive to the ship?

Yes. Port Canaveral charges $20 per day plus tax for parking, paid by card on arrival. The port does not take parking reservations.
