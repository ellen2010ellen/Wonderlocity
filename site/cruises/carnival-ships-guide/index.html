Carnival ships explained: which class fits your trip

Carnival runs more than two dozen ships in several classes, and the newest Excel-class ships, Mardi Gras, Carnival Celebration, and Carnival Jubilee, carry the biggest attractions in the fleet, while older classes like Conquest and Fantasy cost less and suit shorter, simpler trips.

Quick take

  • Newest shipsExcel class: Mardi Gras, Celebration, Jubilee
  • Biggest attractionsExcel class
  • Budget-friendlyDream and Conquest classes
  • Lowest pricesSunshine and Fantasy classes

Two Carnival cruises can feel like different vacations depending on which ship you board. A 2023 megaship has a roller coaster, a huge water park, and a dozen places to eat. A 1998 ship has a pool, a show lounge, and a lower price. Neither is wrong, but picking blind is the mistake that leaves first-timers disappointed. Here is how the fleet breaks down, from newest to oldest.

Excel class: the newest and biggest

Mardi Gras arrived in 2021, Carnival Celebration in 2022, and Carnival Jubilee in 2023, and they are the largest ships Carnival has built, with Mardi Gras carrying up to about 6,500 guests at full occupancy. Most Excel ships carry the BOLT roller coaster on the top deck, along with the biggest dining and entertainment lineups in the fleet. Two more are coming: Carnival Festivale in spring 2027, which will swap the roller coaster for a larger water park, and Carnival Tropicale in spring 2028. Carnival Celebration is based in Miami on 7-day Caribbean sailings, and Mardi Gras sails from Port Canaveral, moving to shorter Bahamas cruises from late May 2027. Expect the highest fares in the fleet, and book early for school holidays.

Venice class: a different feel

Carnival Venezia and Carnival Firenze joined the fleet from sister brand Costa, so they feel a little different from a classic Carnival ship. Read recent reviews for the exact ship and sailing before you book.

Vista class: the sweet spot

Carnival Vista (2016), Carnival Horizon (2018), and Carnival Panorama (2019) carry about 4,000 guests each, with Panorama at 4,008 and Horizon at 3,960. They are big, modern, and full of attractions, yet they usually cost less than the Excel ships, which makes Vista class the best value for a family that wants a lot of ship without the top price.

Dream class: solid and mid-sized

Carnival Dream (2009), Carnival Magic (2011), and Carnival Breeze (2012) are popular mid-sized ships. Magic and Breeze carry about 3,690 guests. They are comfortable, well-proven ships with good pool decks and dining, and they are a safe middle choice on both price and features.

Conquest and Spirit classes: older and cheaper

The five Conquest-class ships, built between 2002 and 2007, carry roughly 3,000 guests each, and Carnival Freedom (2007, about 2,980 guests) will take over the Norfolk homeport in May 2027. The Spirit-class ships, Spirit, Pride, Legend, and Miracle, are smaller still, with Pride and Legend at about 2,124 guests, plus Carnival Luminosa, which came from Costa. These ships have fewer attractions and a more traditional layout, and that shows up as lower fares and a calmer feel.

Sunshine and Fantasy classes: the lowest prices

The Sunshine class is made up of older ships that were renovated and renamed: Carnival Sunshine was originally Carnival Destiny, launched in 1996, Carnival Sunrise was Carnival Triumph, and Carnival Radiance was Carnival Victory. Carnival Sunshine spent a month in dry dock before it began sailing from Norfolk, Virginia in February 2025. The Fantasy class, Carnival Elation and Carnival Paradise from 1998, is among the oldest and most compact in the fleet, with Elation carrying about 2,130 guests. These suit short getaways and shoppers who put price first.

Check the exact ship

Class is a good guide, but each ship has its own refits and sailings, so read recent reviews for the exact ship and sailing before you book.

So which ship should you pick?

Match the ship to your group. Families with older kids and teens should lean toward Excel and Vista, where the attractions keep everyone busy. Couples and groups watching their budget do well on Dream and Conquest. First-timers testing a 4 or 5 night sailing can start on an older ship and upgrade later. Whatever the class, check the homeport first, because a ship you can drive to saves a flight, and our Carnival Caribbean itineraries guide shows where each ship sails. Common questions

What is the newest Carnival ship?

Carnival Jubilee, built in 2023, is the newest Excel-class ship in service. Carnival Festivale is due in spring 2027 and Carnival Tropicale in spring 2028.

Which Carnival ships are the biggest?

The Excel-class ships, Mardi Gras, Carnival Celebration, and Carnival Jubilee. Mardi Gras holds up to about 6,500 guests at full occupancy.

Does the ship really matter on Carnival?

Yes. Newer ships have far larger attractions and more dining choices, and older ships cost less. Pick the ship before the cabin.

Which Carnival ship sails from Norfolk?

Carnival Sunshine sails from Norfolk, Virginia through mid-May 2027, and Carnival Freedom replaces it from May 15, 2027.
