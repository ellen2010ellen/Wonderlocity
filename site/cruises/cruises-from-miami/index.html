Cruises from Miami: lines, ships, and where they go

PortMiami has ten passenger terminals and serves lines from Carnival, Royal Caribbean, Norwegian and MSC to Virgin Voyages and luxury brands, with Bahamas and Caribbean sailings of every length.

Quick take

  • PortPortMiami, 10 terminals
  • Lines14 listed by Miami tourism
  • Typical tripsBahamas and Caribbean
  • Good forFirst cruises and big ships

Miami has the widest choice of any Florida port. You can pick a giant family ship, a short Bahamas getaway, or a long luxury sailing, and flights into Miami are easy from most of the country.

Who sails from Miami

The Greater Miami tourism site lists Azamara, Carnival, Celebrity, Cunard, Holland America, MSC, Norwegian, Oceania, Princess, Regent Seven Seas, Royal Caribbean, Seabourn, Silversea and Virgin Voyages. That is a lot of range, from mass-market to ultra-luxury.

These are the ships most families look at first. They have the most pools, slides, and clubs for kids, so compare the kids programs and age groups on each line's site before you book.

Adults only and quieter options

Virgin Voyages ships sail from Miami, often with a stop at its Beach Club at Bimini, but Virgin sails for adults only, so it is not an option for a family trip. Luxury brands such as Oceania, Seabourn, Silversea and Regent suit couples and travelers who want smaller ships.

Where the ships go

Miami sailings include Bahamas trips year-round, Caribbean cruises of seven nights and longer, some one-way voyages to Europe, and longer luxury itineraries. Cunard sails roundtrip Caribbean cruises of 9 to 21 nights.

Plan a night in Miami

Arrive the day before, and use the evening for a beach walk. It is easier than racing to the terminal after a morning flight.

Before you book

Look at the embarkation day and terminal, and ask how far your hotel is from the port. Compare total cost with gratuities and drinks, not only the fare, and read our Carnival review for a plain look at what a week really costs.

Common questions

What cruise lines sail from Miami?

Miami tourism lists 14, including Carnival, Royal Caribbean, Norwegian, MSC, Celebrity, Princess, Holland America and Virgin Voyages.

Is Miami good for a family cruise?

Yes, because it has many large family ships from Royal Caribbean, Carnival, MSC and Norwegian. Check each line's kids program before you book.

Can kids go on Virgin Voyages?

No. Virgin Voyages is adults only, so families should choose another line.
