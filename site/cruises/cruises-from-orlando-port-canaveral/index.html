Cruises from Orlando: the Port Canaveral guide

Orlando does not have its own cruise port: ships leave from Port Canaveral, about 45 minutes east of Orlando International Airport, with Royal Caribbean, Norwegian, MSC, Carnival, Celebrity and Disney all sailing from there.

Quick take

  • PortPort Canaveral on the Space Coast
  • From Orlando airportAbout 45 minutes
  • ParkingFrom $20 per day plus tax
  • Trips3 to 14 nights

Port Canaveral is the easy way to combine a theme park trip with a cruise. You fly into Orlando, spend a few days at the parks, then ride east to the ship. Here is how it works and what to plan before you book.

Which lines sail from Port Canaveral

Cruise Critic lists Royal Caribbean (including Utopia of the Seas and Star of the Seas), Norwegian, MSC, Carnival, Celebrity and Disney, and Cunard also appears in the lineup. Disney has its own dedicated terminal, which makes the port a favorite for families. Ships and terminals change by season, so confirm yours with your travel advisor or the cruise line.

Where you can go

3 to 4 nightsBahamas, with stops like Nassau, Perfect Day at CocoCay, and Great Stirrup Cay
7 nightsEastern or Western Caribbean, such as St. Thomas, St. Maarten, Cozumel, Grand Cayman, and Ocho Rios
9 nightsSouthern Caribbean, such as Antigua, Barbados, and St. Lucia

Most sailings are seven nights. A short Bahamas cruise is the gentlest first try if you are adding it onto a Florida vacation.

Getting to the port from Orlando

The port says Orlando International Airport is about 45 minutes west of it, via the S.R. 528 toll road. Orlando Melbourne International is about 40 minutes south, and Orlando Sanford is about an hour northwest. Options for the ride include a rental car, a shuttle, a rideshare, a private car, and ground transfers some lines sell. Norwegian, for example, offers airport transfers on select sailings that must be booked at least four days ahead.

Parking, if you drive

The port charges $20 per day plus tax, covering the day you arrive and the day you leave. It does not take parking reservations, you pay on arrival, and cash is not accepted. Garages and lots sit next to the terminals, and parking opens at 10 a.m. on embarkation day. Rates can change, so check the port's page before you go.

Arrive the day before

Flying in the morning of your cruise leaves no room for delays. A hotel night near the port or in Cocoa Beach is cheap insurance, and many hotels there offer shuttles.

Fun things before or after the ship

Kennedy Space Center is about 20 minutes from the port. It has a Rocket Garden of full-size rockets, the Atlantis orbiter, and more than 60 interactive simulators. Cocoa Beach is about 15 minutes south, which makes it an easy beach day.

Combining parks and a cruise

A common plan is four or five days at the parks, a night near the coast, then a short Bahamas cruise. Read our Disney vs Universal guide to choose the parks, and our Disney cruise guide if you want a Disney land and sea trip.

Common questions

Can you cruise from Orlando?

Not directly. Ships sail from Port Canaveral, about 45 minutes east of Orlando International Airport.

How much is parking at Port Canaveral?

$20 per day plus tax, paid on arrival by card. The port does not take reservations or cash.

Which cruise lines sail from Port Canaveral?

Royal Caribbean, Norwegian, MSC, Carnival, Celebrity and Disney all sail from the port, and Cunard also appears in the lineup.
