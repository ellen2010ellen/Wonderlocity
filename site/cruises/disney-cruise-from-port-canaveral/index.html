Disney cruise from Port Canaveral: a land and sea trip

Disney Fantasy, Disney Wish and Disney Treasure are based at Port Canaveral, about 45 to 60 minutes from Walt Disney World, so one trip can include both the parks and a Bahamas or Caribbean cruise.

Quick take

  • Ships basedFantasy, Wish, Treasure
  • Lengths3 to 7 nights
  • From Disney WorldAbout 45 to 60 minutes
  • ParkingAbout $20 per day

For many families, a Disney trip ends with a cruise. Port Canaveral makes that practical, since Disney has a terminal built just for its ships.

The ships

Disney Fantasy, Disney Wish and Disney Treasure are based at Port Canaveral, and Disney Dream and Disney Magic sail from the port on some dates. Fantasy runs 4 and 5 night Bahamas and Western Caribbean cruises. Wish runs 3 and 4 night Bahamas cruises year-round, and Treasure runs 7 night Eastern and Western Caribbean sailings. Check the current schedule, because it changes by year.

Getting there from Disney World

Port Canaveral is about 45 miles east of Orlando. It is roughly a 45-minute to one-hour drive from Disney World. Options include a rental car, a shuttle, a rideshare, and Disney Cruise Line ground transfers. If you drive, parking by the terminal is about $20 per day, paid on arrival.

How to plan the days

Park daysFour to five days at the parks first
TransitionOne easy night or a morning drive to the port
Cruise3 to 4 nights is easiest for a first try
Book the cruise early

Disney sailings sell out for school breaks. Hold your dates early, and ask what fits your family's budget.

Is it worth it?

A Disney cruise costs more than most mainstream lines. If your kids love Disney, a short sailing can feel like another park day with a beach stop. If budget matters most, compare it with a Carnival or Royal Caribbean cruise from the same port using our family cruise guide.
