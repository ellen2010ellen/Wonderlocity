Orlando and Disney

A week in Orlando for a family of four costs $3,800 - $7,500 in 2026, including round-trip flights, a near-park hotel, 4-day Disney World tickets, and a rental car.

Orlando is where a clear plan saves the most money, because tickets, hotel location, and park days interact. Families overspend by buying more park days than they will use and by staying too far from the action. The fix is matching the ticket to the trip.

Best time to go

Avoid summer heat and crowds and the December holidays for the highest prices. Late January to February, late August to September, and early December outside the holidays give lower crowds and better rates. Check the Disney crowd calendar before locking dates.

Common questions

How much does a Disney World trip cost for a family of four?

Budget $3,800 to $7,500 for a week in 2026 once flights, a near-park hotel, 4-day tickets, and a car are included. Park-day count and hotel choice move the number the most.

How many days do you need at Disney World?

Four park days suits most families with young kids. Buying more days than you will use is the most common Orlando overspend.

When is the cheapest time to go to Disney World?

Late January and February, late August into September, and early December outside the holidays bring lower crowds and rates. Summer and the December holidays are the priciest.

Is it cheaper to stay on-site or off-site at Disney?

It depends. On-site adds convenience and early park access, off-site can be cheaper for larger families. We compare both for your dates before you book.
