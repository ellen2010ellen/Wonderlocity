Punta Cana

A week all-inclusive in Punta Cana runs $1,400 - $3,200 per person in 2026, covering round-trip flights from the US East Coast, a 4-star beachfront resort, all food and drinks, and airport transfers.

Punta Cana is the easiest big beach win from the East Coast: nonstop flights are short, the all-inclusive value is strong, and you can go premium or budget on the same stretch of sand. The price gap comes down to season, resort tier, and how far in advance you book.

Best time to go

Best value is late April to early June and November, when weather is still good and rates drop after peak. Avoid the late summer hurricane window if you want certainty. Christmas, New Year, and spring break are the most expensive weeks of the year.

What a Wonderlocity Punta Cana trip includes

  • Round-trip flights from your home airport, priced both nonstop and one-stop
  • A 4 or 5-star all-inclusive matched to your budget and travel style
  • Airport transfers and resort selection based on adults-only versus family

Common questions

Is Punta Cana all-inclusive worth it?

For most beach trips, yes. Bundling flights, hotel, food, and drinks into one all-inclusive rate is usually cheaper than paying separately, and it makes budgeting simple. The exception is travelers who plan to eat off-resort often.

When is the cheapest time to go to Punta Cana?

Late April to early June and the first half of November give the best mix of low rates and good weather. Prices spike around the December holidays, spring break, and US long weekends.

How many days do you need in Punta Cana?

Five to seven nights is the sweet spot. Shorter feels rushed after the travel day, and longer is great if you want pure rest rather than touring.

Do I need a passport for Punta Cana?

Yes. US citizens need a valid passport to enter the Dominican Republic, and it should not expire within the period your airline requires.
