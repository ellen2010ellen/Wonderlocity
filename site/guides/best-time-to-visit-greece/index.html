The best time to visit Greece by island and budget

The best time to visit Greece is mid-May to mid-June and September to early October, when the sea is warm, the islands are fully open but not packed, and good hotels run $90 - $180 a night instead of the $200 - $400 they charge in peak summer.

Greece is really two destinations wearing one name: the whitewashed islands everyone pictures, and the mainland of Athens, Delphi, and the Peloponnese. They peak at different times, and the price gap between a June week and an August week on the same island is bigger than almost anywhere in Europe. Here is how to pick your window by season, by island, and by budget.

Late spring, mid-May to mid-June

The first sweet spot. The sea has warmed enough to swim, every ferry route and taverna is open, wildflowers are still on the hillsides, and the crowds that swamp Santorini and Mykonos in July have not arrived. Hotel rates sit 30 to 50 percent under peak, and you can still book a caldera-view room weeks out rather than months. If you only get one recommendation, this is it.

Peak summer, July and August

Hot, windy in the Cyclades thanks to the meltemi, and as expensive as Greece gets. The famous islands are shoulder to shoulder, sunset spots in Oia require staking out a place an hour ahead, and the same room that cost $150 in May lists for $350. That said, peak has its case: the longest days, the warmest sea, every beach club and boat trip running, and the best nightlife on Mykonos and Ios. If you go, book four to six months ahead and lean toward quieter islands.

September and early October

The other sweet spot, and the better one for swimmers. The Aegean holds its summer heat, so the sea in late September is warmer than in June, while the August crowds vanish almost overnight after the first week of September. Prices drop back to shoulder rates, the light turns golden, and restaurant owners have time to talk again. By mid-October, island hotels start closing for the season, so aim for the front half of the window.

Winter and early spring, November to April

The cheapest time by far, and the wrong time for an island trip. Most island hotels and restaurants close, ferry schedules thin to a trickle, and the sea is cold. But it is a genuinely good window for the mainland: Athens sightseeing without lines, Delphi and Meteora in crisp quiet air, and city hotel rates at $60 - $120 a night. If your Greece trip is museums and ruins rather than beaches, February to April is an underrated bargain.

The best time by island

Santorini is best in May, early June, and late September, when the caldera views come without the cruise-ship crush. Mykonos wants June through early September if nightlife is the point, and shoulder weeks if it is not. Crete has the longest season, swimmable from May into late October, and its size absorbs crowds better than the small Cyclades. Rhodes and the Dodecanese run warm and busy all summer and stay pleasant into October. Corfu and the Ionians are greener, milder, and best May through September. Naxos, Paros, and Milos follow the Santorini calendar at half the price.

The best time by budget

For a 7-night trip for two, covering mid-range hotels, ferries or short flights, food, and sightseeing, these are realistic 2026 ranges by season.

Peak, July and August$3,500 - $6,500 per couple
Shoulder, mid-May to mid-June and September$2,200 - $4,000 per couple
Off-season mainland trip, November to April$1,400 - $2,500 per couple

Flights from the US follow the same curve. A New York to Athens round trip that costs $1,100 in late July routinely drops to $650 - $850 in shoulder months and under $600 in winter.

When to book

For peak summer on Santorini or Mykonos, book flights and hotels four to six months out, since the best-located rooms sell first and never discount. For shoulder season, two to three months is usually enough, and last-minute deals appear in May and late September. Common questions

What is the cheapest time to visit Greece?

November to April is cheapest, with mainland trips running $1,400 - $2,500 per couple for a week, but most island hotels close. The best value with the islands open is mid-May to mid-June and September, at $2,200 - $4,000 per couple.

When is the sea warm enough to swim in Greece?

From roughly mid-May through late October. September is the warmest-water month because the Aegean holds its summer heat, which is why it beats June for swimmers.

When should I avoid Santorini and Mykonos?

Late July and August, when cruise crowds, the meltemi wind, and peak prices all hit at once. The same trip in early June or late September costs 30 to 50 percent less and feels far calmer.
