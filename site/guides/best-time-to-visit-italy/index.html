The best time to visit Italy, by region and budget

The best time to visit Italy is mid-April to early June and mid-September to October, when the weather is warm, crowds thin, and prices sit below the summer peak.

Italy rewards shoulder-season travel more than almost anywhere in Europe. You get long, mild days, lighter crowds at the big sights, and hotel rates well under July and August. Here is how to choose your window.

Spring, April to June

The sweet spot. Comfortable temperatures, blooming countryside, and manageable crowds before the summer rush. Book ahead for the late-May long weekends.

Summer, July and August

Hot, busy, and most expensive, and many locals leave the cities in August. Great for the coast and lakes, harder for sightseeing in Rome and Florence.

Fall, September and October

The other sweet spot. Warm seas into September, harvest season inland, and softening prices. October is ideal for cities and food regions.

By region

For the Amalfi Coast and the islands, May to early October. For Rome, Florence, and Venice, spring and fall. For the Dolomites, summer hiking or winter skiing.

Common questions

What is the cheapest time to visit Italy?

November to March outside the holidays is cheapest, but the value-with-good-weather window is mid-April to early June and mid-September to October.

When is the best weather in Italy?

Late spring and early fall give warm, settled days without the August heat. The coast stays swimmable into September.

When should I avoid visiting Italy?

August is hottest, busiest on the coast, and quietest in the cities, since many businesses close while locals take holidays.
