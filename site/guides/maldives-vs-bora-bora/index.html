Maldives vs Bora Bora for a honeymoon: cost and feel

A 7-night honeymoon costs $4,500 - $14,000 per couple in 2026 in either the Maldives or Bora Bora, with the Maldives offering deeper resort choice and lower entry prices, and Bora Bora delivering its iconic turquoise lagoon and Mount Otemanu views in a more compact, single-island setting.

Both are bucket-list overwater honeymoons, and both photograph like a dream, so the real decision comes down to budget, how far you want to fly, and the kind of feel you are after. Here is the honest comparison, with the numbers up front.

How the two compare on price

The Maldives spans a wider price range because it has everything from value 4-star resorts to ultra-luxury private islands, so the entry point is lower. Bora Bora has fewer resorts and pricier flights from the US, which pushes the floor higher. For a 7-night overwater honeymoon with round-trip US flights, here is the 2026 range.

Maldives, 7-night overwater honeymoon$4,500 - $11,000 per couple
Bora Bora, 7-night overwater honeymoon$7,000 - $14,000 per couple
Round-trip US flights, Maldives$1,400 - $2,600 per couple
Round-trip US flights, Bora Bora via Tahiti$2,000 - $3,400 per couple

What the Maldives does best

The Maldives is a scattering of more than a hundred resort islands, each one its own private world, so you can match the trip to your budget without giving up the overwater villa. The water is a luminous, glassy blue, the house reefs are excellent for snorkeling straight off your deck, and the sheer choice means strong shoulder-season deals. It suits couples who want the overwater dream at the widest range of prices, and who like the idea of having an entire small island to themselves.

What Bora Bora does best

Bora Bora is a single dramatic island ringed by a vivid lagoon, with the green peak of Mount Otemanu rising at its center, so the scenery has a grandeur the flat Maldives atolls do not. The overwater bungalow was effectively born here, the lagoon is calm and swimmable, and French Polynesian culture, food, and warmth give the trip a distinct character. It suits couples who want that one unmistakable view, a mountain-and-lagoon backdrop, and a more concentrated sense of place.

Getting there from the US

The Maldives is a long haul east, usually one stop through the Middle East or Asia, then a seaplane or speedboat to your island, with total travel often 22 to 30 hours from the East Coast. Bora Bora is reached by flying to Tahiti, most easily from Los Angeles in about 8 hours, then a short 50-minute hop to Bora Bora, which is far simpler from the US West Coast. If you live in the West, Bora Bora is the easier journey, while the Maldives can be the same effort from either coast.

Which feels more romantic

This is genuinely close and personal. The Maldives wins on seclusion and water clarity, since your villa often sits over an empty turquoise flat with no one in sight. Bora Bora wins on the postcard view, with bungalows framed against the lagoon and Mount Otemanu, and on a livelier sense of being somewhere with culture and a small town nearby. For pure barefoot privacy, lean Maldives. For drama and an iconic backdrop, lean Bora Bora.

Cheapest time to go

The Maldives is cheapest and quieter from May to early November, the off-peak season, with a higher chance of short rain, while December to April is the dry, peak, and priciest stretch. Bora Bora flips this slightly: its dry season runs May to October, which is also high season for weather, so the best value windows are the shoulder weeks of April, May, and late October before and after the peak. For both, booking six to nine months ahead matters more than the exact month.

The verdict

Choose the Maldives for the widest price range, the clearest water, and the most private overwater feel, especially if you are flying from the East Coast where the journey is similar either way. Choose Bora Bora for the single most recognizable honeymoon view, an easier trip from the West Coast, and a stronger sense of local culture, accepting a higher starting price. At a matched budget the Maldives usually buys a more luxurious villa, while Bora Bora buys a backdrop nothing else replicates.

Common questions

Is the Maldives or Bora Bora cheaper for a honeymoon?

The Maldives is usually cheaper to start, from about $4,500 per couple for a week versus roughly $7,000 for Bora Bora, mainly because it has more value resorts and cheaper flights. At the top end both reach $11,000 to $14,000 for ultra-luxury stays.

Is the Maldives or Bora Bora better for a honeymoon?

Both are world-class. The Maldives offers more seclusion, clearer water, and a wider range of prices, while Bora Bora offers the iconic lagoon and Mount Otemanu view and an easier trip from the US West Coast.

How long is the flight to the Maldives versus Bora Bora?

From the US East Coast the Maldives is often 22 to 30 hours with one stop, while Bora Bora is about 8 hours from Los Angeles to Tahiti plus a 50-minute hop, making it far quicker from the West Coast.
