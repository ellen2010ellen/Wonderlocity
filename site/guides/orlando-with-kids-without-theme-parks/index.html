Orlando with kids: what to do besides theme parks

Orlando has plenty for kids beyond the parks, including a 110-acre gator park, a four-floor science center, an aquarium with 250 species, and Kennedy Space Center about an hour away.

Quick take

  • Best forRest days between parks
  • Day tripKennedy Space Center, about 1 hour
  • AnimalsGatorland and SEA LIFE
  • IndoorScience Center and aquarium

A week of theme parks wears kids out, and it wears out budgets too. These are the places families use for the in-between days.

Animals and wildlife

Gatorland is a 110-acre wildlife preserve with a zipline over the alligator breeding marsh. SEA LIFE Orlando Aquarium has 250 species across nine exhibits. Giraffe Ranch runs a guided safari that ends with a chance to feed a giraffe.

Science and space

The Orlando Science Center has four floors of interactive exhibits, along with live fish and alligators. Kennedy Space Center is about an hour from Orlando, and its bus tour drives past the launch pad and shows a real rocket.

Gardens and quiet time

Leu Gardens offers kid-friendly scavenger hunts, plus holiday light displays and rotating exhibits through the year. It is a calm choice for a break day.

Mix it up

Plan one non-park day for every two park days. Kids stay happier, and you spend less on tickets.

Common questions

What can kids do in Orlando besides theme parks?

Try Gatorland, SEA LIFE Orlando Aquarium, the Orlando Science Center, Leu Gardens, or a day at Kennedy Space Center.

How far is Kennedy Space Center from Orlando?

About an hour by car.

Is the Orlando Science Center good for kids?

Yes. It has four floors of hands-on exhibits and live fish and alligators.
