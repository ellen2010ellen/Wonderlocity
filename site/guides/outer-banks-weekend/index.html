An Outer Banks weekend from Virginia Beach: costs and plan

An Outer Banks weekend from Virginia Beach costs $350 - $900 for two people in 2026, covering two nights in a hotel or small rental, gas for the roughly 90-minute drive, meals out, and a couple of paid attractions.

The Outer Banks is the easiest great weekend you can take from Virginia Beach. It is close enough to leave after breakfast on Saturday and still have a full beach day, far enough to feel like a real trip, and cheap enough outside peak summer that a spontaneous weekend does not need a budget meeting. Here is the route, the costs, and a two-day plan that fits it all in without rushing.

Getting there from Virginia Beach

The drive is about 90 minutes to Kitty Hawk on the most direct route, south on US-168 through Chesapeake, across the state line, then NC-158 to the Wright Memorial Bridge. It is a two-lane highway for long stretches, so summer Saturday traffic can push it past two hours. Leave before 8 am on summer weekends and you will roll onto the beach road while everyone else is still queued at the bridge. Off season, the drive is genuinely easy at any hour.

Where to base yourself

For a two-night trip, stay in the central towns: Kitty Hawk, Kill Devil Hills, or Nags Head. They put you within ten minutes of the Wright Brothers Memorial, Jockey's Ridge, the fishing piers, and the widest choice of restaurants, and they have the deepest supply of hotels and small rentals. Duck and Corolla to the north are prettier and quieter but add 30 to 45 minutes each way and skew toward week-long house rentals. Hatteras and points south are wilder and wonderful, but they are better saved for a three-night trip, since the extra hour of driving eats a weekend.

What a weekend costs in 2026

These ranges cover two people for two nights, with the low end for an off-season hotel weekend and the high end for a summer weekend in a nicer oceanfront room.

Hotel or small rental, 2 nights$180 - $550
Gas from Virginia Beach, round trip$25 - $40
Food, two days of casual meals out$100 - $220
Attractions and parking$20 - $90

The swing is almost entirely the room. The same oceanfront hotel that asks $260 a night on a July Saturday lists for $90 - $120 in April or late October, which is why shoulder season is the local's move.

A two-day plan that works

Day one: arrive by mid-morning, drop bags, and take the beach while the light is good. In the late afternoon climb the dunes at Jockey's Ridge State Park, which is free, for the best sunset on the northern beaches, then eat in Nags Head or Kill Devil Hills. Day two: start at the Wright Brothers National Memorial when it opens, walk the first-flight line before the tour buses, then either browse the shops and duck donuts in Duck or drive south past Oregon Inlet to Pea Island for empty-beach walking and birdlife. Lunch on the water, one last beach hour, and you are home by dinner.

The best time of year to go

May, early June, September, and October are the sweet spots. The ocean is swimmable from late May through October, the crowds and rates peak from mid-June to mid-August, and September in particular gives warm water, open restaurants, and hotel prices a third below July. Winter weekends are the bargain, with $80 - $120 oceanfront rooms and empty beaches, as long as a windswept walk sounds better to you than a swim.

Worth knowing before you go

Book summer Saturdays two to three months out, since the central-beach hotels fill first, while off-season trips can be booked the same week. Beach parking is free at most public accesses in Kitty Hawk and Kill Devil Hills but fills by 10 am in summer. And check the events calendar before you pick your weekend, because fishing tournaments and the marathon in November can quietly double room rates.

Common questions

How far is the Outer Banks from Virginia Beach?

About 60 miles and 90 minutes to Kitty Hawk on US-168 and NC-158, though summer Saturday bridge traffic can stretch it past two hours. Leaving before 8 am avoids the worst of it.

How much does an Outer Banks weekend cost?

$350 - $900 for two people for two nights in 2026, covering the room, gas, meals, and attractions. The room drives the range, with off-season rates at less than half of July prices.

What is the best time for an Outer Banks weekend?

May, early June, September, and October. September is the standout, with warm ocean water, thinner crowds, and hotel rates roughly a third below peak summer.
