Road trip from Virginia Beach to Orlando with kids

Virginia Beach to Orlando is about 769 miles and roughly 14.5 hours of driving, so most families split it into two days with an overnight in South Carolina or Georgia and add one or two kid-friendly stops.

Quick take

  • DistanceAbout 769 miles
  • Drive timeAbout 14.5 hours, no stops
  • Best planTwo days with one overnight
  • Good stopsSavannah, Walterboro, Fayetteville

Driving to Orlando from the Virginia Beach area is a long day, but it has real upsides for a family: you bring everything, you have a car when you get there, and you can turn the trip into a vacation of its own. The key is to treat it as two easy days rather than one hard one.

The numbers

The drive is about 769 miles and roughly 14.5 hours without stops, which is why a stop for the night makes the trip so much calmer. Most families take I-64 to I-95 south. Plan around meals, gas, and restroom breaks with kids, and add time for traffic near big cities.

Where to break the drive

Think of the trip in three chunks: a first leg through Virginia and North Carolina, a middle leg through South Carolina and Georgia, and a short last day into Florida. Families who prefer a shorter day two often sleep in Georgia or South Carolina, so the Orlando arrival comes before dinner.

Kid-friendly stops along I-95

Fayetteville, NCU.S. Army Airborne and Special Operations Museum, indoors
Walterboro, SCWalterboro Wildlife Sanctuary, boardwalks and trails
Savannah, GARiverfront walk and The Pirates' House
Cape Canaveral, FLKennedy Space Center and Canaveral National Seashore

In Fayetteville, the Army Airborne and Special Operations Museum is indoors, so it works in any weather. In Walterboro, the wildlife sanctuary has boardwalks and trails, which is a good way to stretch legs. In Savannah, walk the historic riverfront, and kids can climb the spiral staircase at The Pirates' House up to a pirate gift shop and restaurant. Near Cape Canaveral, Kennedy Space Center and Canaveral National Seashore, with 24 miles of undeveloped beach, are worth a detour if you have time.

Make the first stop short

Pick a stop within two or three hours. It sets a relaxed pace and gives the kids a first win before the long middle stretch.

Keeping the car calm

Pack snacks in a reachable bag, download shows and music before you leave, and bring a few games that need no screen. Give older kids a job, like tracking the map or choosing the next stop, and give younger ones a small surprise for each leg.

Drive or fly?

Driving makes the most sense for a bigger family, for anyone who wants a car in Orlando, and for people who like a slower start to the vacation. Flying saves a day each way, so it fits a short trip better. Either way, read our Orlando on a budget guide before you book hotels and tickets.

Common questions

How far is Virginia Beach from Orlando by car?

About 769 miles, or roughly 14.5 hours of driving without stops.

Can you drive from Virginia Beach to Orlando in one day?

It is possible, but it is a very long day with kids. Most families split it into two days with an overnight in South Carolina or Georgia.

What are good kid-friendly stops on the way to Orlando?

Fayetteville, North Carolina for the Army Airborne museum, Walterboro, South Carolina for the wildlife sanctuary, and Savannah, Georgia for the riverfront and The Pirates' House.
