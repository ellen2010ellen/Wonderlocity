Family vacations

A week-long family vacation in 2026 typically costs $3,500 - $8,000 for a family of four, with all-inclusive beaches and Orlando the most popular picks.

Family trips live or die on logistics, and the budget hinges on a few big choices: drive or fly, all-inclusive or theme park, and how many paid days you really need.

All-inclusive beaches

The lowest-stress family week. Kids clubs, pools, and food included means fewer decisions and a known total. Punta Cana, Cancun, and the Riviera Maya lead for value.

Orlando and Disney

The classic, and the easiest place to overspend. Matching the ticket to the right number of park days is the single biggest saver.

Where families save

Travel in shoulder weeks, stay near the action rather than on it, and skip the add-ons kids will not remember. We build the plan so you pay for what you will use.

Common questions

How much does a family vacation cost?

Plan $3,500 to $8,000 for a family of four for a week in 2026. All-inclusive beaches and Orlando are the most popular, and park-day count drives the Orlando total.

Is all-inclusive or Disney better for families?

All-inclusive is lower stress and easier to budget, Disney is the bucket-list experience. We can price both for your dates.

When is the cheapest time for a family trip?

Outside school holidays. Late spring, early fall, and early December bring lower crowds and rates.
