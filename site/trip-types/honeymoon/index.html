Honeymoons

A 7-night honeymoon in 2026 ranges from $3,000 to $12,000 per couple, with the Maldives, Bora Bora, and Santorini at the top, and the Caribbean and Mexico offering the best value.

A honeymoon is the one trip worth getting right, and the budget swing is huge. The destination sets the floor, the resort sets the ceiling, and a few smart choices on timing and transfers decide where you land.

Where to splurge

Spend on the room and the view. An overwater villa or a caldera-edge suite is the memory. Skip the extras that photograph well but add little.

Best value honeymoons

The Caribbean and Mexico deliver a luxury feel for far less than the Indian Ocean or the South Pacific. A Riviera Maya or St Lucia honeymoon can feel five-star at a Maldives-adjacent dream for half the price.

Timing matters

Shoulder season trims hundreds to thousands off the same trip. We build the plan around the cheapest good-weather window for your destination.

Common questions

How much should I budget for a honeymoon?

Plan $3,000 to $12,000 per couple for a week in 2026. The Maldives, Bora Bora, and Santorini sit at the top, the Caribbean and Mexico offer the best value.

When should we book a honeymoon?

Six to nine months out for peak destinations, both to lock rates and to access honeymoon perks. We watch for price drops after booking.

What is the most romantic affordable honeymoon?

St Lucia, the Riviera Maya, and Santorini in shoulder season give a luxury feel without Maldives-level prices.
